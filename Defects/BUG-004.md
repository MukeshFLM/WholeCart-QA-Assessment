# BUG-004: Out-of-stock products are displayed in buyer catalogue and search results

**Module:** Buyer – Product Catalogue / Search  
**Severity:** Medium  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Open  
**Requirement Reference:** BR-02

### Description
The application displays products with zero available stock in the buyer catalogue search results. Buyers can view products that are unavailable for purchase.

### Preconditions
1. Seller is logged in.
2. A product has zero available stock.
3. Buyer account is available.

### Test Data

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Product | Golden Sona Masoori Rice |
| SKU | SKU-0018 |
| Seller stock | 0 pieces |

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Login as seller2 (FreshFarm Foods).
2. Navigate to product inventory.
3. Identify Golden Sona Masoori Rice (SKU-0018).
4. Verify that available stock is 0 pieces.
5. Logout from seller account.
6. Login as buyer1.
7. Navigate to Catalogue.
8. Search using SKU-0018.
9. Observe the search results.

### Expected Result
Products with zero available stock must not be displayed in the buyer catalogue or search results.

### Actual Result
The application displays Golden Sona Masoori Rice (SKU-0018) in the buyer catalogue even though its available stock is zero.

### Business Impact
- Buyers can discover unavailable products.
- Creates a poor buying experience.
- Buyers may attempt to purchase products that cannot be fulfilled.

### Evidence
1. Seller inventory screenshot showing SKU-0018 with 0 pieces.
2. Buyer catalogue screenshot showing SKU-0018 in search results.

### Requirement Reference
BR-02: Products with no stock are never shown in the catalogue or in search results.
